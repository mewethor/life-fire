Life Fire

Turn your day into a quest. Small real-life challenges, every day, that you can actually finish.

Download for Android Open in your browser

Free. No ads. No account. For ages 12 and up.

What a day looks like

One main quest

A bigger challenge, like a 30-minute walk without your phone. Worth 100 XP.

Three mini quests

Quick wins that take a few minutes. Worth 25 XP each, and you can swap one.

Ten bonus quests

Optional extras when you want more. Worth 10 XP each.

A mind game

Five questions a day: riddles, logic and things you actually study.

Streaks and a shop

Finish your main quest to keep your streak. Spend coins on streak freezes and avatars.

Made for you

Tell it if you are a student, a university student or a parent, and your quests fit. Your day resets at midnight where you live.

Install on Android

  1. Tap Download for Android and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the file. If Android says it can’t install from this source, tap Settings and allow it for your browser or Files app.
  3. If Google Play Protect warns you, tap Install anyway. The app isn’t in the Play Store.
  4. Open Life Fire and answer a few questions to set up your hero.

On iPhone or any browser

  1. Tap Open in your browser.
  2. In Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. In Chrome on Android, open the menu and tap Install app.

Your privacy. Your name, age and progress stay on your device. There are no ads and nothing is sold or tracked. The Android app checks GitHub every few hours to see if a newer version is ready, and shows a banner when there is.
